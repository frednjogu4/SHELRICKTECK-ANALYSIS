    How the confidence score is computed (quality score — NOT a probability of profit)
    Technical confluence score, 0–100: 15 base + 7 per confirming timeframe (up to 5) + up to 25 for bias strength (5×|avg bias|, capped) + 20 when the multi-timeframe signal is confirmed; overall cap 93 (WAIT signals capped at 45). Index CFD reads use 50 + 8×|signal score|, capped 90. Formula is fixed and shown here so the number is explainable and stable — it is a setup-quality/confluence score, not a calibrated chance of profit.

        XML bot library

        Uploaded Deriv strategies — reviewed before use
        Risk first. Recovery-style bots (Martingale) can drain a balance quickly. Every imported bot is scanned for stake progression and rated — and no bot can arm without a connected broker.
        Calculator Lab — risk education
        Every tool here is educational and hypothetical: you supply the assumptions, the math is shown, nothing is sent to any broker, and nothing here predicts or guarantees anything. Market history comes from the live Deriv public feed and is labelled per panel. Past behaviour of a market never guarantees its future.
        1 · Percentage change
        How much did a value move, and what does it take to move back? Notice the asymmetry: a −50% move needs +100% to recover.
        Method: (to − from) ÷ |from| × 100. Recovery % = what the “to” value needs to return to “from”.
        2 · Hypothetical profit & loss
        Your assumptions only — enter an imagined entry/exit and costs. This is arithmetic on your numbers, not a forecast, and it never places an order.
        Method: gross = (exit − entry) × units × direction; costs = fees on both sides' notionals + slippage × 2 sides; margin = notional ÷ leverage. Leverage multiplies gains and losses alike.
        3 · Historical drawdown (real market history)
        Buy-and-hold drawdown from actual candles: how far the price fell below its running peak, and for how long. Limitations: one instrument, one period, one granularity — past drawdown does not bound future drawdown.
        No history loaded yet.
        Source: —
        4 · Volatility comparison (real market history)
        Realized (statistical) volatility from actual candle returns, annualized so different instruments are comparable. Limitations: a statistical description of the past — not a prediction, and it changes with every new candle.
        Method: stdev of log returns × √(periods/year). Data: Deriv public feed, last 400 candles per instrument.
        What leverage, fees and slippage really do
        • Leverage borrows buying power: 20× turns a 1% market move into a 20% move on your margin — in either direction. Losses can exceed the margin you posted.
        • Fees are charged on every side of every trade whether the idea worked or not; high-frequency plans must first overcome their own costs.
        • Slippage is the gap between the price you wanted and the price you got — it widens in fast markets and around news.
        • Losses are part of trading: no calculator, signal, or system eliminates them. Anyone promising otherwise is not being honest with you.

        Auto-trade — sniper entries

        Scans live MTF signals, enters only at ≥ target confidence, manages exits — and once armed it keeps hunting until you stop it (survives reloads; pauses on session expiry and auto-resumes when the session returns).
        Sniper mode
        Either way the sniper never stacks on itself (max 1 sniper position). Server-side stake/trade/daily-loss caps always apply.
        Exit strategy
        Deriv locks short forex binaries — they always run to expiry. Target exit buys 1h contracts, whose resale typically unlocks mid-life, so the sniper can genuinely close at your target. With Fast expiry you get the earliest possible result; with Target exit you get real early closes.
        Prefer early-exit markets
        OFF (default): the sniper hunts the normal universe. ON: it hunts only early-exit-friendly markets (synthetics — where Deriv usually allows resale and Close genuinely stops the trade). Guidance only: Deriv decides per contract and it's verified live after each buy.
        Daily loss guard (all trades — manual, news, sniper)
        Stopped-out $ of closed losses per day (default 25, 0 = off). When today's closed P&L reaches −this, manual orders, news Execute and the sniper all stand down until tomorrow. The server-side caps remain the hard backstop.
